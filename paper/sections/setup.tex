\section{Experimental setup at 125M parameters}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Model and data.} A Llama-style decoder~\citep{touvron2023llama}: 12 layers, width 768,
12 heads, SwiGLU hidden width 2048, rotary position embeddings, pre-norm RMSNorm, no biases, tied
input and output embeddings, vocabulary 50{,}304, context 1024; 123.6M parameters. Training data
are the first 2.5B tokens of the shuffled training documents of FineWeb-Edu \texttt{sample-10BT}
\citep{penedo2024fineweb}, tokenized with the GPT-2 tokenizer; a fixed hash of the document identifier
assigns 20M-token validation and test splits from disjoint documents (checksums in the repository).
As a check of the data and loss pipeline, the OpenAI GPT-2 (124M) checkpoint~\citep{radford2019language}
scores \GptTwoOurVal{} nats on our validation split, against \GptTwoPublished{} reported on the
FineWeb-Edu validation shard of build-nanogpt~\citep{karpathy2024buildnanogpt}.

\paragraph{Training.} 240 sequences of 1024 tokens per step (245{,}760 tokens): confirmatory runs take
10{,}172 steps (2.49987B tokens, every training sequence once), tuning runs 2{,}500 steps (614M
tokens). The learning rate warms up linearly for 5\% of the steps and decays by a cosine to 10\% of
its peak; gradients are clipped at global norm 1; decoupled weight decay 0.1 applies to the hidden
matrices only. The hidden matrices use the optimizer under test; embeddings and norms use AdamW
($\beta=(0.9,0.95)$, no weight decay) with the same peak learning rate and schedule as the hidden
matrices (the AdamW baseline is one optimizer, so its tuned $\beta_2$ applies to every parameter).
bfloat16 compute, float32 master weights and optimizer state. A seed fixes the initialization and the data order and is shared by all optimizers.

\paragraph{Implementation on a TPU v4-32.} All three optimizers are written in JAX
\citep{bradbury2018jax} as pure per-matrix steps, verified against the PyTorch references (per-step
agreement to about $10^{-12}$ in float64 for Gimbal; SOAP against the official implementation). The
hidden matrices are stored in two buckets of equal shape (48 of $768\times768$ and 36 of
$768\times2048$); the optimizer state of each bucket is sharded across the 16 chips, each chip
updates its own matrices with batched products, and only the parameter increments are gathered.
Every optimizer gets the same layout, batching, sharding and precision (all optimizer products in
float32). Attention uses the Pallas flash-attention kernel~\citep{dao2022flashattention}: with
$512\times512$ tiles a training gradient step takes \AttnFlashTuned{}~s against \AttnXla{}~s with
XLA attention and \AttnFlashDefault{}~s with the kernel's default tiles.

\paragraph{Step time.} Measured as the median, over 20-step blocks, of the mean step time in a
block (so that SOAP's refresh every 10 steps and Gimbal's frame move every four are included),
the training step takes \StepAdamw{}~ms with AdamW, \StepSoap{}~ms with SOAP and \StepGimbal{}~ms
with Gimbal, \StepGimbalOverSoap\% more than SOAP. Gimbal's frame move on the 2048-dimensional side
(retraction, orthogonality check and Newton--Schulz polish in float32) is the difference; moving the
frame every two steps or every step would cost \StepKtwoOverSoap\% and \StepKoneOverSoap\% more
than SOAP.

\paragraph{Tuning (equal budgets).} Each optimizer received the same budget: seven peak learning
rates on a factor-two grid around its reference default (AdamW $10^{-3}$, SOAP and Gimbal
$3\cdot10^{-3}$), two seeds each, at the 614M-token horizon, followed by its most sensitive
secondary knob at the selected rate (AdamW $\beta_2\in\{0.95,0.98,0.999\}$, SOAP's factor memory
$\beta_s\in\{0.9,0.95,0.99\}$, Gimbal's rate $\alpha\in\{0.025,0.05,0.1\}$), two seeds each.
Selection used the full validation split; the test split was not read. Gimbal runs with SOAP's
momentum $\beta_1=0.95$, so that the two differ only in the frame. Its first tuning trials used
Adam's $\beta_1=0.9$ and were unstable around the end of the warm-up at learning rates
$\ge1.5\cdot10^{-3}$; short diagnostic runs showed that SOAP is unstable with $\beta_1=0.9$ as well,
and that neither moment transport nor per-step frame moves remove the instability
(\cref{app:diag}). Those trials are reported but were not used: Gimbal was re-tuned with the full
budget. Selected values:
AdamW learning rate \LrAdamw{} with $\beta_2=\KnobAdamw$; SOAP \LrSoap{} with $\beta_s=\KnobSoap$;
Gimbal \LrGimbal{} with $\alpha=\KnobGimbal$ (\cref{app:tuning}).

## Diagnostics

* Premise. The noise-corrected non-separability index of Gimbal's flow variances (median over the matrices of a bucket, final step) is 0.0026, 0.0028 (attention, seeds 2 and 3) and 0.0040, 0.0046 (MLP), against the predeclared premise threshold 0.05: the rotated variances are close to separable, as on the small model (F-030). The empirical-Bayes factor is accordingly small (0.031, 0.034, 0.024, 0.028): Gimbal's variance weights are almost the separable fit, i.e. close to KL-Shampoo's.
* Frame quality at the end (D-005 item 8): on held-out validation gradients each run's own frame beats a pooled-factor frame fitted to 16 fresh gradients in 8/8 matrices for Gimbal and 7/8 for SOAP.
* Frame quality at step 1,000 (exploratory, D-009, seed 2): own frame better than the fresh pooled frame in 3/4 matrices for Gimbal and 0/4 for SOAP. On the evaluation curves (every 250 steps) Gimbal was ahead of SOAP at 40/41 (seed 2) and 36/41 (seed 3) evaluations, by up to 0.040 nats, and SOAP caught up as the learning rate decayed (`analysis/results/loss_curves.png`). AdamW, too, was below SOAP at 22/41 and 18/41 evaluations, by up to 0.038 nats, so the mid-training lead mostly reflects SOAP's higher learning rate (observation added after the test evaluation; the decision is unchanged).
* Stability: no loss spikes (D-005 definition) in any run; frames orthogonal to about 1e-6 throughout.

## Classification (protocol §4)

* Against SOAP: **effect smaller than noise** (mixed signs over the two seeds, a mean difference far below the threshold and of the order of the seed-to-seed spread) and **mechanism prediction falsified** at its premise (the non-separability the likelihood frame exploits is nearly absent); plus a **wall-clock loss** (Gimbal's step is slower, as measured in Phase 04, G4.2).
* Against AdamW: the predicted advantage of a Kronecker-frame method is confirmed.

## What happens next (D-011)

Protocol §9 asks to report the negative result with its evidence rather than to search for a configuration that reverses it; the owner instructed the work to be completed without questions, so no further repair cycle is run and the result is reported as it is. The test split is evaluated once, after this file, for the paper.

The next experiments with the most information, not run here:

1. Gimbal's tuning was not complete in one dimension: its rotation rate was selected at the edge of its grid (faster frames kept helping). A larger rate (and the early-phase schedule of the frame, which the tuning runs show to be fragile at high learning rates) is the cheapest test of whether the remaining gap to SOAP closes.
2. KL-SOAP, which beat Gimbal on the small model under separable statistics, at 125M.
3. Settings where the rotated variances are measured to be non-separable (other architectures or layers; the last MLP projection has the largest pooled-frame index among the probed matrices), where the theory predicts the likelihood frame to matter.

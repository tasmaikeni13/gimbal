# Phase 09 — The paper

## Purpose

Turn the theory document, the Lean development and the experimental evidence into a complete,
self-consistent paper whose every claim is supported at the stated strength.

## Depends on

Phase 08 (`claims.md`, `decision.md`, generated figures), `theory/gimbal_theory.md`, `formal/`,
`research/literature/frontier.md`.

## Produces

`paper/main.tex` (+ `paper/sections/*.tex`, `paper/refs.bib`, `paper/figures/` generated by
`analysis/` scripts), `paper/main.pdf`, `paper/README.md` (build instructions).

## Structure

1. Abstract — the claim exactly as allowed by `claims.md`.
2. Introduction — SOAP's frame/eigenvalue inconsistency; contributions.
3. Background — SOAP, Shampoo, KL-Shampoo, Muon family; notation.
4. Method — KRD model, objective $J$, the Gimbal flow, algorithm box, cost.
5. Theory — identifiability, efficiency, local rate, retraction, equivariance; what is machine-checked
   (Lean) and what is not; conjectures labelled as such.
6. Monte Carlo and small-scale analysis — Phase 02 results.
7. Language-model experiments — Phase 07/08 results with uncertainty and wall-clock methodology.
8. Ablations and mechanism diagnostics.
9. Related work — from the frontier document, including the closest works (KL-SOAP, real-time SOAP,
   TurboSoap, ARO, COSMOS, Clean) and the BSS/CPC sources of the mechanism.
10. Limitations — scale (one model size), seeds (two), hardware (TPU v4), horizon (1× Chinchilla).
11. Reproducibility statement — commands, configs, hashes, data checksums, Lean build.
Appendix — full proofs, Lean theorem index, hyperparameter tables, tuning budgets, all runs.

## Rules

* Numbers come from generated tables; no number is typed by hand.
* Every theorem in the paper carries its evidence label; Lean names are cited for L5 results.
* Every related-work statement is checked against the primary source (`literature-frontier`).
* The adversarial novelty pass is repeated on the final method description before submission
  (search date recorded).

## Exit gate

* G9.1 `make -C paper` (or `latexmk`) builds without errors or undefined references.
* G9.2 Claim audit: each sentence with a result maps to a `claims.md` row.
* G9.3 Novelty pass re-run and dated.
